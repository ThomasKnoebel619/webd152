About Pacific Trails Resort

Welcome to Pacific Trails Resort a peaceful getaway on California’s beautiful North Coast. Enjoy the natural beauty of the coast while relaxing in comfort and style. Every detail of our resort is designed to give you a memorable escape from the everyday.

Our Story

Nestled amidst the dramatic cliffs and pristine forests of the North Coast, Pacific Trails Resort was founded with a passion for preserving and sharing this region's natural splendor. Our vision was to create a retreat that harmonizes with its stunning surroundings, providing a space where guests can forge a deep connection with nature. We believe that true luxury lies in the quality of the experience, the authenticity of the setting and the impeccable service we provide.

The Pacific Trails Experience

At Pacific Trails Resort, your stay is defined by the freedom to choose your own adventure or simply unwind in style. Our amenities and activities are thoughtfully designed to cater to every mood.

  • Relax and Indulge: Unwind in our lodge, where you can enjoy complimentary appetizers and beverages while taking in the serene coastal views. As evening falls, savor nightly fine dining that showcases local flavors with an ocean backdrop that changes with the setting sun.
  • Rejuvenate Your Senses: Take a dip in our heated outdoor pool or let the day's worries melt away in our soothing whirlpool. The sound of the waves and the fresh ocean air provide the perfect setting for complete relaxation.
  • Explore and Discover: Our resort is your gateway to exploration. You can venture out on your own or join one of our guided tours to discover the hidden gems of the coast. With 5 miles of dedicated hiking trails and direct access to an adjacent state park, there are endless opportunities to explore on your own or with one of our guided hikes.
  • Connect with Nature: Experience the power of the Pacific Ocean up close with complimentary ocean kayaks available for guest use. For those who enjoy a slower pace, join our guided bird-watching trips at sunrise several times a week to witness the vibrant local wildlife.
